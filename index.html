CTR / Engagement Opportunity Scoring

▸
Paper → Repo →

Built and deployed an empirical CTR-by-position benchmark and opportunity-scoring pipeline on a 79M-row real search-performance warehouse, querying directly via DuckDB over remote Parquet. Identified and documented a real, large-sample anomaly in the benchmark curve rather than smoothing it away, caught and fixed an impression-weighting bug before shipping, and separately validated a decline-prediction model with a client-grouped split — reporting an honest negative result instead of a misleadingly good naive-split number. On the fixed data, logistic regression beat random forest at every K for precision@K.
